Dhadkan Is Crowned Winner of ‘MTV Hustle 5 – Apna Homeground’

날짜: 2026-10-11

요약:

Dhadkan Is Crowned Winner of ‘MTV Hustle 5 – Apna Homeground’
The Sonipat artist was award a grand prize of ₹1 crorer, with rappers WhysoKai as first runner-up and Harmeet as second runner-up
Sonipat rapper Dhadkan aka Daksh Pendharker has been announced as the winner of hip-hop talent show MTV Hustle 5 – Apna Homeground today, Oct. 11, 2026.
Dominating with incisive rap, but also incorporating Haryanvi folk influences and his Marathi roots, Dhadkan took home the winner title and a grand prize of ₹1 crore on the fifth season of the show, with rappers WhysoKai in second place and Harmeet in third place.
Over weeks of performances featuring 25 artists across 20 episodes, MTV Hustle 5 – Apna Homeground brought back hip-hop/pop star Badshah as judge, as well as Squad Boss EPR. New Squad Bosses this season included Hustle alums such as MC Square, Agsy and Paradox, with the show also inviting guest judges ranging from Raja Kumari to Ikka to King, Dino James and Kunal Khemu, among others.
Dhadkan says in a statement, “It still feels unreal jab bhi main sochta hoon ki ek time pe maine apni notes mein is moment ko manifest kiya tha… and cut to the winner of Hustle Season 5 is Dhadkan! Yeh journey has been nothing short of crazy, full of new experiences, learnings and a beautiful new family.” He adds, “Asli safar toh ab shuru hua hai! (The real journey has just started)”
The winner was part of Paradox’s squad, called Para Troopers. Paradox, who was runner-up in season 2 of the show, says in his statement, “Seeing Dhadkan lift the trophy is a proud moment and a reminder of how much talent and hard work this season has brought to the stage. Every artist on Hustle 5 - Apna Homeground has had their own journey and Dhadkan has truly made his mark with his performances and dedication. It’s great to see him take home the title and I’m excited to see his journey grow and what he does next with this prize money and the opportunities that come with it.”
Badhsha added in his statement that “every season of Hustle unearths authentic voices” and called Dhadkan’s time on the show “exceptional right from the start.” The hitmaker added, “I hope this is just the beginning of bigger things for him. Big love and respect to all the hustlers this season; each one brought something irreplaceable to the culture.”
